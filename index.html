Security and reliability for LLM agents

I teach in the Department of Software Engineering at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology. I study memory leakage, workflow reliability, and delegated permissions in LLM agents, as well as the code they generate.

I’m affiliated with URA Lab (Assoc. Prof. Tho Quan) and RAISE Lab (Dr. Xuan-Bach Le).
