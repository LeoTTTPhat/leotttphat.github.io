I am currently a lecturer (faculty member) in the Faculty of Computer Science & Engineering, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), VNU-HCM . I work with URA Lab, led by Assoc. Prof. Tho Quan, and RAISE Lab, led by Dr. Xuan-Bach Le.

Research interests and background

I earned my BEng (Honors) and MEng (by Research) in Computer Science at HCMUT. Before focusing on my academic career, I worked as a software engineer and technical lead on AI and big data systems.

My research spans AI Safety, Trustworthy AI, AI/Software Security, Data Privacy, and Machine Learning. I develop methods to make AI and software systems secure, private, and reliable by combining probabilistic modeling, formal methods, data mining, secure software engineering, and privacy-preserving techniques. My recent work focuses on Verified and Auditable Agentic Systems: what they leak from memory, how reliably they complete multi-step tasks, and how to keep the permissions they are given within bounds.

Research directions

01

Agent Security & Reliability

Auditing cross-session memory leakage, estimating reliability in multi-step workflows, and enforcing purpose-bound delegation.

Topics and related work in agent security and reliability

I quantify what information leaks between sessions in shared graph memories and estimate the reliability of multi-step agent pipelines. I also design runtime controls for the capabilities that agents receive when they act on someone else’s behalf.

Topics

  • Cross-session memory leakage
  • Agent workflow reliability
  • Absorbing Markov chains
  • Purpose-bound delegation
  • Runtime capability enforcement

Related work

Trustworthy AI Evaluation & Verification

Checking that AI results hold up: auditing whether evaluations support their claims, and verifying AI-assisted discoveries, table joins, and retrieved evidence.

Topics and related work in trustworthy AI evaluation and verification

I test when benchmark and evaluation results support the claims made from them, controlling for data leakage, selection effects, and audit quality. I also build verifiers for what AI-assisted analysis produces, from data-mining discoveries and table joins to the evidence behind retrieval-augmented answers.

Studying model-generated code across many samples and using taint-based slicing to detect malicious software dependencies.
