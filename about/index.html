Academic background

I am a lecturer in the Department of Software Engineering, Faculty of Computer Science & Engineering, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), VNU-HCM. I work with URA Lab, led by Assoc. Prof. Tho Quan, and RAISE Lab, led by Dr. Xuan-Bach Le.

I earned my BEng (Honors) and MEng (by Research) in Computer Science at HCMUT. Before focusing on my academic career, I worked as a software engineer and technical lead on AI and big data systems.

I lead work on LLM agent security and reliability, building on my research in differential privacy, distributed systems, and software supply chain security. I am seeking a PhD in trustworthy AI, software engineering, and security and privacy.
